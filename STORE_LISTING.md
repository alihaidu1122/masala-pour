## Full description
Masala Sort is a cozy color-sorting puzzle inspired by spice jars.

**How to play**
• Tap a jar to pick it up, tap another to pour
• Stack matching spices until every jar is pure
• Undo, restart, or earn helpers with rewarded ads

**Features**
• 300 hand-tuned & generated levels
• Daily challenge that changes every day
• Color-blind friendly symbols
• Smooth pour animations and satisfying feedback
• Optional Remove Ads (interstitials only — helpers stay free)

No pay-to-win. Progress saves on your device.
